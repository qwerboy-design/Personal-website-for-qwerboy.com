Overview

Submit and confirm leave requests in LINE, connecting calendars, leave records, and administrator notifications.

Problem

Leave messages, calendars, and employee records are fragmented, making manual transcription prone to duplicates and omissions.

Solution

Applicants submit and confirm requests in LINE. Power Automate validates identity and inputs, creates Outlook events, conditionally updates Excel, and records status and failed steps.

My Role

Leave workflow design, field contracts, cross-system automation, and operational handoff.

Architecture Context

LINE → webhook/Power Automate → SharePoint identity and leave state → Outlook/conditional Excel → notifications

Capabilities

  • Identity binding and leave confirmation
  • Outlook calendar and conditional Excel records
  • Event deduplication, error tracking, and recovery

Tech Stack

  • LINE Messaging API
  • Power Automate
  • SharePoint
  • Outlook / Excel
