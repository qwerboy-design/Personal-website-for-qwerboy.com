Overview

Combines flight search and fare organization to compare travel options across providers.

Problem

Flight data differs across providers, making prices, times, and restrictions harder to compare.

Solution

Provides a search workspace and provider manager with normalized results and snapshots. Daily Watch remains feature-gated; scheduling and notifications require separate verification.

My Role

Architecture Context

Search inputs → provider manager/cache → normalization/sorting → workspace/price snapshots

Capabilities

  • Multi-provider normalization and sorting
  • Price snapshots and Daily Watch foundation

Tech Stack
