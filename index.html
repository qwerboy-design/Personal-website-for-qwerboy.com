Products 產品

Health-Care-Assistant

An AI health and medical-record assistant. It combines AI chat, FHIR data import and privacy (PHI) redaction to help organize healthcare information, with model selection and human review kept in the loop.

AI 健康與病歷助理。整合 AI 對話、FHIR 資料匯入與隱私（PHI）遮罩，協助整理醫療資訊，支援模型切換並保留人工審閱。

FlightBot

A flight search and monitoring bot. It combines flight search with fare organization across providers, normalizes and sorts results, and keeps price snapshots, with a Daily Watch foundation for monitoring fares.

航班搜尋與監控機器人。整合多個來源的航班搜尋與票價整理，正規化並排序結果、保存價格快照，並具備 Daily Watch 票價監控架構。
